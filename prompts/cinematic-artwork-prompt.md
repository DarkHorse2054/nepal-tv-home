## Final prompt

Asset type: atmospheric 16:9 landscape background for a polished television home screen, ideally 3840x2160, no UI or text.
Primary request: an immersive, richly detailed Nepal-inspired Himalayan dusk landscape with subtle Hindu temple architecture, cinematic photographic realism and art direction.
Scene: enormous naturally shaped snow-covered Himalayan peaks across the middle and right, dramatic alpine valley with layered mist, a small traditional Nepali Hindu pagoda temple on a distant right-side terrace, tasteful amber oil-lamp and window lights. The temple is an imagined architectural scene, not a claim to depict a specific landmark. Calm indigo dusk, deep teal shadows, warm copper and gold highlights, realistic mountain textures and atmospheric depth. A faint crescent moon is acceptable but no oversized celestial objects.
Composition for an actual TV interface: left 45 percent is deep, softly textured indigo sky and dark atmospheric valley with subdued detail to sit behind a large white clock; brightest peaks and temple lights occupy the middle-right from 30 to 65 percent image height; bottom quarter fades naturally into deep graphite and shadow for a five-app dock. Keep a quiet dark area in the upper-right for a date card. Scenery should still be beautiful and dramatic, neither a nearly black blank canvas nor a bright wallpaper.
Look: sophisticated cinematic matte photography, physically plausible landscape, restrained vivid color, premium streaming-interface backdrop, intricate authentic-looking Nepali tiered pagoda silhouette, soft volumetric haze, natural materials, crisp detail where useful.
Constraints: landscape 16:9, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no application icons, no interface, no people, no deity statues or faces, no Tibetan Buddhist flags or unrelated religious symbols, no cartoon/vector/low-poly style.
