# AirPlay

Open **Home → Tools → AirPlay** for connection help. This guide uses the receiver built into an AirPlay-compatible television; it does not turn the Fire TV Stick into an AirPlay receiver or install a third-party receiver.

1. Connect the Apple device and television to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. On iPhone, iPad or Mac, open **Control Center → Screen Mirroring**.
3. Select the television and enter the AirPlay code shown on its screen, if requested. On a Samsung television, choose the Samsung receiver rather than the Fire TV Stick.

The television receives the stream directly. The companion remote and its Mac relay do not need to remain running for AirPlay. Some video services restrict AirPlay or screen mirroring.

## Samsung setup

Samsung lists 2020 Crystal UHD televisions, including the TU series, as AirPlay 2 compatible. On those models, enable **Settings → General → Apple AirPlay Settings → AirPlay**. Other model years may place this under **Connection**. The television may request Smart Hub terms or a Samsung account during setup. Keep its on-screen pairing-code protection enabled.

The guide does not toggle this setting, launch an undocumented Samsung app, or claim that the TV is connected. No Samsung AirPlay app ID or remote settings deep link is assumed. A receiver discovered on the local network demonstrates advertised availability; it does not prove that an iPhone or Mac has paired, that mirroring works, or that a protected video service permits playback. On other televisions, check the manufacturer's AirPlay support and setup instructions first.

## Tested installation

On the tested Samsung TU7000, local AirPlay receiver discovery and an owner-confirmed iPhone Screen Mirroring connection were verified. Mac casting, latency, audio quality and protected streaming-service playback were not separately tested. The guide does not monitor a casting session or report connection status.
